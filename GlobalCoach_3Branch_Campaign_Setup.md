## 5. LOCATION TARGETING — no overlap between the 3 branches

Don't rely on guessing exact km distances between Manikonda, Madhapur, and Panjagutta — some of these branches may only be 6-10 km apart in Hyderabad, and radius circles that size can easily overlap and cause your own 3 campaigns to bid against each other (self-competition, which quietly inflates CPCs).

Do this instead for **each** campaign:
1. **Target:** Locations → Advanced search → enter that branch's exact address → set a radius (start with 4-5 km — tight enough to stay hyperlocal, since students generally pick the nearest institute).
2. **Location option:** set to **"Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations"** — not "Presence or interest." This stops people merely searching *about* Hyderabad from elsewhere from triggering your ads.
3. **Exclude the other 2 branches' catchment:** in the same Locations settings, go to the **Exclude** tab and add a radius (same 4-5 km) around the other two branch addresses. Do this in all 3 campaigns, cross-excluding the other two.

This guarantees zero overlap regardless of the actual distance between them, and is more reliable than trying to calculate exact non-overlapping radii by hand.

4. Turn off **Search Partners** and **Display Network** if either is checked — search-only for lead gen like this.
5. Set device bid adjustments after 1-2 weeks of data (course-seekers often research on mobile but call/enroll after seeing it on desktop — don't assume mobile-only is best).

## 6. LAUNCH SOP CHECKLIST

### Before you touch "Publish" (Day 0 — do NOT skip these)
- [ ] **Fix conversion tracking first.** Confirm the GTM-TC9RMPLK Phone Call Click and WhatsApp Click conversion actions are created and firing (this was an open issue as of last week). If your new campaigns launch with bad/incomplete conversion signals, whatever bid strategy runs will optimize on the wrong data — same root problem repeated 3x.
- [ ] Decide bidding strategy: start on **Manual CPC or Maximize Clicks** for the first 1-2 weeks (no conversion history yet in these new campaigns) → switch to **Maximize Conversions** once each campaign has ~15-20 logged conversions → consider **Target CPA** only after 30+ conversions in 30 days, consistently.
- [ ] Set a realistic daily budget per campaign (start conservative, e.g. ₹800-1,200/day per campaign, and scale the ones that perform).
- [ ] Location radius + cross-exclusions set per Section 5.
- [ ] Ad schedule: run during hours your staff can actually answer calls/WhatsApp (e.g. 9 AM-8 PM) — no point paying for a lead at 2 AM nobody responds to for 8 hours.
- [ ] Sitelinks, callouts, call extension, location extension added to every ad group.
- [ ] Negative keyword list (Section on negatives above) applied to all 3 campaigns.
- [ ] Confirm each branch's landing page loads fast, is mobile-friendly, shows that branch's address/phone, and has the enquiry form near the top (not buried).
- [ ] If possible, get a separate tracked phone number per branch so you can tie actual admissions back to which campaign/branch drove them — not just "a lead."

### First 30 days — weekly
- [ ] Pull the Search Terms report **twice a week** and add new irrelevant terms to negatives immediately — don't wait for the report to accumulate waste.
- [ ] Check each keyword's ad relevance/quality status weekly. Anything stuck on "Below average" for 2+ weeks — rewrite the RSA or reconsider the keyword.
- [ ] Check Impression Share lost (budget vs. rank) per campaign — tells you whether to raise budget or fix ad/bid quality.
- [ ] Get actual admission numbers from the client weekly, not just "leads." A cheap lead that never enrolls isn't actually cheap — track cost-per-admission, not just cost-per-lead.
- [ ] After 2 weeks of data, review device and time-of-day performance and adjust bid adjustments accordingly.

## Things you were missing (added above, flagging again so you don't lose them)
1. **Conversion tracking fix is the single highest-leverage item** — it affects every other number you'll look at from these campaigns.
2. **Negative keyword list** — without this, your new campaigns will develop the exact same "General" dilution problem within a few weeks.
3. **Bid strategy sequencing** — new campaigns have zero history; don't launch straight into automated bidding.
4. **Budget split 60/40 FICO/MM**, not even — MM has underperformed FICO consistently in this account so far.
5. **Cost-per-admission tracking**, not just cost-per-lead — this is what will actually satisfy whoever is judging "performance" above you.
6. Consider **audience signals** (In-Market: Education, or a custom segment of people who've searched competitor institute names / SAP job portals) once campaigns are live — this helps automated bidding find the right people faster.
